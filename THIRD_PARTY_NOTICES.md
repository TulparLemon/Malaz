### GPLv3 Source Obligation & LGPLv3 Section 3 Notice for Qt / PySide6

In Nuitka standalone builds, compiled Python modules and Qt/PySide6 libraries are distributed together in a self-contained folder. Rather than relying on LGPLv3 Section 4 relinking mechanics, Malaz explicitly exercises **LGPLv3 Section 3** (and Qt/PySide6's dual **GPL-3.0** licensing option) to convey the combined work under **GPL-3.0-or-later** (`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/pyside6*/licenses/LICENSE.GPLv3`). PySide6, shiboken6, Qt, and `pcodedmp` are used unmodified from their upstream releases (corresponding upstream sources available via PyPI, <https://code.qt.io/>, and <https://github.com/bontchev/pcodedmp>). Redistributors of binary builds must ensure access to both the Malaz source tree and corresponding upstream package sources as required by GPLv3 Section 6.

### Why `signature-base` Is Not Bundled (Verified License Audit)

Auditing `Neo23x0/signature-base` (documented in `rules/yara/signature_base/LICENSE-DRL-1.1.txt` and `rules/yara/signature_base/LICENSES-EINZELREGELN.txt`) identified two concrete reasons against bundling it in a `GPL-3.0-or-later` distribution:

1. **Non-Commercial Per-Rule Licenses (`CC BY-NC 4.0`):** Out of 5,255 audited rules, 11 carry `CC BY-NC 4.0` (alongside rules under `OGL` and vendor-specific notices). A non-commercial restriction constitutes an "additional restriction" under GPLv3 Sections 7 and 10 and cannot be combined into a `GPL-3.0-or-later` release.
2. **DRL 1.1 Attribution Requirements:** The repository-level *Detection Rule License (DRL) 1.1* requires preserving `author` metadata, source URIs, and license text both when redistributing rules and in detection outputs.

When a user downloads `signature-base` locally via `scripts/update_signature_base.bat`, `author` metadata inside the local `bundle.yarc` as well as `LICENSE-DRL-1.1.txt` and `ATTRIBUTION.txt` are preserved on disk.

## External Tools (Not Bundled — Invoked as Separate OS Processes)

ClamAV (`clamscan.exe` / `clamdscan.exe`), Windows Defender (`MpCmdRun.exe`), 7-Zip (`7z.exe`), Detect It Easy (`diec.exe`), and `capa` are invoked strictly via command-line execution (`shell=False`) as separate operating-system processes and are **neither linked with nor distributed alongside Malaz**. Their respective upstream licenses apply to the user's local installation.
