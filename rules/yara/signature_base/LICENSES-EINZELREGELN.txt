Per-Rule License Metadata Audit in signature-base (5,255 Rules)
===============================================================
Counted across the `license` metadata fields of 5,255 compiled rules:
- 11x CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial restriction; incompatible with GPL-3.0 bundling)
- 8x OGL (Open Government Licence)
- 6x Volexity
- 6x SigmaHQ DRL
- 4x CC BY 4.0
- 5x BSD
- 2,392 rules without an explicit `license` metadata field

The upstream repository is published under Detection Rule License (DRL) 1.1,
while individual rules may carry additional restrictions such as CC BY-NC 4.0.
Accordingly, Malaz never bundles signature-base in source or binary releases.
